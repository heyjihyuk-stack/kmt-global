SHEET 02 · What we do

One supplier,
start to finish

We don’t pass you on to a production site. We sign the contract, set up production and remain accountable to the end.REV A

From quotation to after-sales support, you deal with one party: KMT Global. We take on the eight tasks below and remain accountable for them. For each one, we state up front what you receive.

  1. 02.1

    Engineering review & quotation

    What KMT Global does

    We review your drawings (2D and 3D) and specifications for castability, material, machining scope and inspection requirements. With your written approval, we confirm feasibility with the production site, then issue a quotation.

    What you receive

    • Engineering review comments
    • Proposed production route
    • Quotation
  2. 02.2 · assigning production sites

    Production setup

    What KMT Global does

    We choose the production site that fits the size, volume, material and certification requirements. We combine casting, machining and finishing into one production plan.

    What you receive

    • Production route and who handles each step
    • One consolidated schedule

    For partner foundries and machining partners, we tell you the company name and location after an NDA is signed, for that order only.

  3. 02.3

    Pattern & tooling management

    What KMT Global does

    We set the specifications for patterns, core boxes and tooling, and keep records of how they are made, modified and stored. We also agree and handle the receipt of customer-supplied patterns.

    What you receive

    • Pattern specification
    • Modification and storage records
    • Written terms of ownership and storage
  4. 02.4

    Quality control & inspection

    What KMT Global does

    We set inspection criteria to your requirements. For orders at the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate, Korea Metal Tec’s quality team inspects the parts, and we check the inspection records and documents. For orders at partner production sites, the production site inspects the parts, and we cross-check the inspection results against the lots. Witnessed or third-party inspection is arranged case by case, and inspection methods and locations are set in an inspection plan for each part after the order is placed.

    What you receive

    • Inspection plan (after the order)
    • Manufacturer’s inspection documents
    • Pre-shipment inspection verification record issued by KMT Global
  5. 02.5

    Production & delivery management

    What KMT Global does

    We check the schedule and progress at the production site. If we see a possible change or delay, we tell you first and propose alternatives.

    What you receive

    • Progress reports
    • Advance notice and alternatives if the schedule changes
  6. 02.6

    Machining & finishing

    What KMT Global does

    Machining is carried out by external machining partners. We review the machining partner’s process plan and how it will hold the tolerances, and check the post-machining measurement results. We also set finishing specifications, such as fettling, painting and rust protection, to your requirements.

    What you receive

    • Machined parts
    • Post-machining measurement results
    • Confirmed finishing specification
  7. 02.7

    Export logistics & documents

    What KMT Global does

    We prepare export packing, customs clearance and shipping documents. Delivery terms such as Incoterms® are agreed case by case, and proof of origin is also reviewed case by case.

    What you receive

    • Packing specification
    • Shipping documents such as the commercial invoice and packing list
    • Result of our proof-of-origin review
  8. 02.8

    One point of contact in three languages

    What KMT Global does

    One point of contact handles communication on engineering, quality and delivery in Korean, English and Japanese.

    What you receive

    • One named contact person
    • Records of agreements and changes

SHEET 03 · Engineering review

When your drawing arrives,
we start with an engineering review

Much of a casting’s quality and cost is decided at the design stage. Before quoting, KMT Global reviews your drawing from an engineering standpoint. Where a change would improve the part, we propose it and explain why. The review is carried out by engineers of our affiliate KOREA METAL TEC CO., LTD., who also work for KMT Global.

  1. Drawing received
  2. ①Drawing & castability review
  3. ②Casting method & pattern review
  4. ③Material & quality engineering
  5. ④Engineering support through machining & assembly

Tap a point or hover over it to read the note.

  1. Abrupt changes in wall thickness are prone to shrinkage defects. We check thickness transitions and the solidification sequence, locate the areas at risk and propose design changes.

  2. We find as-cast (unmachined) surfaces where the draft is missing or insufficient. We separate machined from unmachined surfaces and propose the draft each one needs.

  3. Based on the casting tolerances and the machining datum, we propose a machining allowance for each surface. Too little leaves as-cast skin; too much adds machining time and tends to expose defects.

  4. We review the path of the molten iron and the location and size of the risers that feed shrinkage. If a riser would sit on a machined or visible surface, we also propose moving it.

  5. At the drawing stage, we check hardness measurement locations, dimensional datum points and the scope of non-destructive testing and note them in the review report. They are set in the inspection plan after the order is placed.

For all five items, we record the location and our proposal in the drawing review report (FIG. 18). The circled numbers ①–④ refer to the four capabilities below.

①DFM

Drawing & castability review (DFM)

In a design for manufacturability (DFM) review, we check castability, wall thickness, draft and machining allowance. Where there is a risk of shrinkage or distortion, we propose design changes.

Deliverables

Drawing review report (with proposed improvements)

②Casting method

Casting method & pattern design

For orders at the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate (furan no-bake), Korea Metal Tec’s engineers design the casting method (gating and risering design), including the location and size of gates and risers, and the parting and construction of patterns and cores. For orders at partner foundries (automatic molding), we review the production site’s casting method and tooling design.

Deliverables

Casting method drawing · pattern and core design specification (Goryeong foundry orders, after the order) · review results of the production site’s casting method (partner foundry orders)

③Material & quality

Material & quality engineering

For orders at the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate, Korea Metal Tec’s engineers set the target chemical composition; for orders at partner foundries, we review the production site’s composition criteria. We check the composition against spectrometric analysis results and set inspection criteria for hardness and graphite structure. If casting defects occur, we analyze the root cause and work on improvements with the production site.

Deliverables

Material control criteria · inspection plan (after the order) · defect root-cause analysis and improvement report

④Machining & assembly

Engineering support through machining & assembly

Machining is done by external machining partners. We review the machining partner’s process plan (machining sequence and datums) and measurement results. Taking your assembly requirements into account, we check whether casting tolerances, machining allowance and the machining process can hold the drawing tolerances.

Deliverables

Machining process plan · tolerance review results · post-machining measurement results

You receive our review as a written report

For each location on the drawing, we record the finding and risk, our proposal and the rationale, so you can approve or hold each item. Below is the template with the fields only; the actual content differs for every drawing.

    SHEET 07 · Quality & documents

    What is inspected and which documents you receive —
    agreed up front

    Inspection items and document types are agreed at the quotation stage. For orders at the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate, Korea Metal Tec’s quality team carries out the inspections and KMT Global checks the inspection records and documents. For orders at partner production sites, the production site carries out the inspections and KMT Global cross-checks the results against the lots. Witnessed or third-party inspection is arranged case by case.

    07.1Inspection items 07.2Document flow 07.3Certifications 07.4Material cross-reference 07.5Report template

    07.1 Inspection items

    • C · Si · Mn · P · S · Mg

      Chemical composition

      We check C, Si, Mn, P and S, and Mg for ductile iron, against the production site’s chemical analysis results (e.g. optical emission spectrometry, OES).

    • Rm · Rp0.2 · A

      Mechanical properties

      Tensile strength Rm (gray and ductile iron); 0.2% proof strength Rp0.2 (yield strength) and elongation A (ductile iron). The test-piece type (separately cast, cast-on, or cut from the casting) is agreed in advance.

    • HBW

      Hardness

      Brinell hardness (HBW). The measurement locations are set in the drawing or purchase order.

    • ISO 945-4 · ASTM E2567

      Microstructure

      Graphite form, nodularity and matrix structure. Nodularity values depend on the measurement method, so the method is specified as well (ISO 945-4, ASTM E2567, etc.).

    • ISO 8062-3

      Dimensions

      Dimensional inspection to the drawing. The measurement method and the casting tolerance standard (ISO 8062-3, etc.) are agreed for each part.

    • VT

      Visual inspection

      Casting defects, fettling condition, painting and rust protection.

    • UT · MT · PT

      Non-destructive testing (if specified)

      Where the drawing or purchase order specifies non-destructive testing (NDT), such as ultrasonic UT, magnetic particle MT or penetrant PT, we confirm and agree the method, acceptance criteria and who performs it (production site or an external inspection body) at the quotation stage.

    Other tests specified separately in the drawing or purchase order, such as hydrostatic and leak testing, are handled the same way: we confirm and agree the method and who performs it at the quotation stage.

    07.2 Document flow

    KMT Global never alters a production site’s test results. We record what we have verified in a pre-shipment inspection verification record issued by KMT Global.REV A

    Inspection document types — EN 10204

    EN 10204 defines the types of inspection documents for metallic products. Which type you receive is decided at the quotation stage, after we confirm which documents the production site can issue.

    EN 10204 inspection document types
    TypeContent
    2.1 Declaration of compliance with the orderStatement by the manufacturer that the products comply with the order, without test results
    2.2 Test reportManufacturer’s report that includes results of non-specific testing
    Inspection certificate 3.1Results of specific tests on the lot, validated by the manufacturer’s authorized inspection representative, independent of the manufacturing department
    Inspection certificate 3.2As 3.1, and also validated by the purchaser’s authorized inspection representative or an inspector designated by official regulations

    Four facts to check
    when widening your supplier base

    Casting production in Japan and Germany is declining, while US casting sales are projected to grow. Korea is a manufacturing base with free trade agreements (FTAs) in force with the US, the EU and Japan. We have summarized the facts with their sources, for reference when you consider a second source (dual sourcing).

    • Japan

      4.51 million t

      Japan’s casting production in 2024 was 4.51 million t, down about 7% from the previous year and about 17% from 2019.

      Source · Foundry-Planet, 2026-07-20

    • Germany

      −8%

      According to the German Foundry Association (BDG), German production of iron and steel castings in the first half of 2025 was 1.34 million t, 8% lower than in the same period a year earlier.

      Source · EUROGUSS 365 · BDG, 2025-10-14

    • USA

      +8.0%

      The American Foundry Society (AFS) projects that US casting sales (by value) will grow by 8.0% in 2026. Sales in 2025 were about USD 53.7 billion.

      Source · AFS, 2026-07-27

    • Korea

      FTA

      Korea has FTAs in force with the US (KORUS FTA, entered into force 2012-03-15), the EU (EU–Korea FTA, provisionally applied from 2011-07-01 and formally entered into force 2015-12-13) and Japan (Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership, RCEP, in force for Korea since 2022-02-01 — the first FTA to which both Korea and Japan are parties). Korea also has an established automotive-parts supply base.

      Source · Korea–US: CRS RL34330 · EU–Korea: EUR-Lex, Council Decision (EU) 2015/2169 (OJ L 307, 2015-11-25) · European Commission, DG Trade · RCEP: Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT)

      Proof of origin and FTA eligibility depend on the part’s HS classification and the rules of origin, so we review them case by case. Tariff rates and classification are decided by the customs authority of the importing country; this page does not state tariff rates or benefit figures.

    SHEET 11 · FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Eleven questions purchasing and quality teams ask first.

    Other questions? Email us →

    01What kind of company is KMT Global?

    KMT Global is a newly established affiliate of KOREA METAL TEC CO., LTD., set up to handle overseas business. KMT Global is accountable for the contract, engineering review, quality and export. Production runs at the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate Korea Metal Tec, at partner foundries and at machining partners. All of them are separate legal entities. KMT Global holds no certification yet; certifications are held in the name of each production site (SHEET 07 · certification table).

    02Who is the contracting party?

    KMT Global. Quotation, contract, invoicing and responsibility for quality and delivery all sit with KMT Global. Even with several production sites, you have one contractual counterparty.

    03Can we know which production site makes our parts?

    For orders at the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate KOREA METAL TEC CO., LTD., we name the production site from the start. For orders at partner foundries and machining partners, we tell you the production site for your order (company name and location) after an NDA is signed; the scope of disclosure is agreed with the production site in advance. Each production site is a separate legal entity, and KMT Global holds the contract with you and the responsibility for quality and delivery. If a production site has to change, we agree it with you first. Beyond our engineering review, we share your drawings only with production sites you approve in writing.

    04Who holds the IATF 16949 certification?

    The IATF 16949-certified partner foundry (that entity) holds it. IATF 16949 is granted to sites that manufacture automotive parts themselves, so KMT Global, which does not manufacture, is not eligible for it. Parts that require IATF 16949 are reviewed for the route through the certified foundry. After an NDA is signed, we tell you the production site for your order and explain how the certificate can be verified.

    05How are our drawings protected?

    If required, we sign an NDA before we receive your drawings. Your drawings are used for the engineering review by engineers of our affiliate KOREA METAL TEC CO., LTD. who also work for KMT Global; beyond that, we share them only with production sites you approve in writing. We tell you the company name and location of the partner production site after an NDA is signed. We use your drawings for that project only.

    06Who owns the patterns and tooling, and where are they stored?

    Ownership, storage location and storage conditions are set in writing at the quotation and contract stage. If the patterns are stored at a partner production site, we tell you its company name and location after an NDA is signed. Customer-supplied patterns are also received on terms agreed with you.

    07Is there a minimum order quantity?

    It depends on the part, the production route and the pattern conditions. On the furan no-bake route (Goryeong foundry of our affiliate Korea Metal Tec), we also review one-offs and small quantities. The automatic molding route suits parts with repeat volumes. Tell us your annual volume and quantity per order, and we will advise you on this together with a suitable route.

    08What are the payment and delivery terms (Incoterms®)?

    They are agreed case by case. Delivery terms are set under Incoterms® 2020 (FCA, FOB, CIF, DAP, etc.).

    09Can you provide proof of origin for FTA purposes?

    Origin depends on the HS classification of the part and the applicable rules of origin, so we review it case by case and advise you. Tariff rates and classification are decided by the customs authority of the importing country.

    10What do you need from us for a quotation?

    Drawings (2D PDF, plus 3D STEP if available), material standard and edition, piece weight or approximate size, annual volume and lot size, machining scope and tolerances, inspection and document requirements, packing and delivery terms, and target schedule. If you need an NDA before sharing drawings, please let us know.

    11Are site visits or audits possible?

    For a visit to or an audit of the Goryeong foundry of our affiliate KOREA METAL TEC CO., LTD., we arrange a date with you. After an NDA is signed, and with that site’s consent, you can carry out an on-site audit of a partner production site. On request, we send you the records of the checks KMT Global has made of the production site.
