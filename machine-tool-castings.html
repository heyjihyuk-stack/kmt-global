Who does what

One contract with KMT Global. Production at the foundry that fits the part.

Most machine-tool castings run at KOREA METAL TEC’s Goryeong foundry, from pattern to machining. Where an operation is outside its range, we arrange a machining partner and tell you before production starts.

  • Patterns — made in-house at KOREA METAL TEC. Ownership and storage terms are agreed in writing at the quote stage.
  • Casting — furan no-bake line in Goryeong; gray iron up to 10 t and ductile iron up to 5 t per casting.
  • Machining — in-house at KOREA METAL TEC; machining partners for work outside its range. KMT Global reviews the process plan and measurement results either way.
  • Inspection documents — chemical composition, hardness, dimensions and visual inspection as agreed; manufacturer’s inspection documents (e.g. EN 10204 3.1) and first article inspection on request.
  • Export — packing, customs clearance and shipping documents. Incoterms® agreed case by case.
  • Drawings — an NDA can be signed before any drawing is sent. Drawings are shared only with production sites you approve in writing.

How a quote works

From your drawing to the first casting

  1. Send a drawing, or ask for an NDA first. 2D or 3D, with material, annual volume and whether machining is needed.
  2. Engineering review. Castability, casting method, material and machining are reviewed before we quote, and we propose improvements where useful.
  3. Quote with the production route. Pattern cost shown separately, with ownership and storage terms, inspection items and documents listed.
  4. Pattern and casting method. Casting method and pattern specifications are fixed with you before tooling starts.
  5. First article. Inspection to the agreed criteria before volume production.
  6. Production and export. One schedule, one point of contact, and advance notice of any change or delay.
